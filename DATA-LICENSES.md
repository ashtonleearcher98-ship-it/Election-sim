# Data attribution and licences

The original application code is MIT. Source-derived datasets and geometry retain their source terms; they are not covered by the code's MIT licence. The in-game source desk supplies individual URLs, dates and notes. The research snapshot is 5 October 2026.

- **Inter-Parliamentary Union, Parline** — global institutional records and latest reported election party distributions. API metadata specifies **Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial–ShareAlike 4.0**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/ . The derived global political dataset in `data/politics.json` and its browser wrapper retains this licence. Attribute IPU/Parline, identify the extraction date and changes, retain the noncommercial/share-alike conditions when redistributing adaptations. https://data.ipu.org/ . Extraction, normalisation, game colours, corrections to explicitly elected seat counts and additional source records are modifications; IPU does not endorse this game.
- **Australian Bureau of Statistics** — ASGS 2025 Commonwealth Electoral Division and State Electoral Division boundaries. Embedded metadata permits **CC BY 4.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Geometry was simplified and converted for browser rendering; mesh-block geography approximates electoral boundaries. https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/standards/australian-statistical-geography-standard-asgs/edition-3-july-2021-june-2026/access-and-downloads/digital-boundary-files . Credit: Australian Bureau of Statistics. AEC boundary geometry is not included: its derivative restrictions are distinct from the ABS source terms.
- **Natural Earth** — generalised world and US state outlines, public domain. https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ . Simplified/filtered for the game; boundaries are contextual and do not express a position on disputed territories.
- **Federal Returning Officer, Germany** — constituency names and election context, **Datenlizenz Deutschland – Namensnennung 2.0**: https://www.govdata.de/dl-de/by-2-0 . https://www.bundeswahlleiterin.de/bundestagswahlen/2025/ergebnisse/opendata.html . Extraction and normalisation are modifications.
- **Australian Electoral Commission and state/territory electoral commissions/parliaments** — public district names, party-register facts, member totals and dated result shares, attributed in the source desk. The project includes extracted factual records, not electoral rolls, campaign logos or AEC boundary products. Source-specific notices continue to apply.
- **UK Parliament and devolved electoral/parliamentary authorities** — public constituency names and current or dated caucus/result facts, with source dates. UK Parliament open data is attributed to UK Parliament; see https://www.parliament.uk/site-information/copyright-parliament/open-parliament-licence/ . Other jurisdictions retain their respective source terms.
- **Elections Canada, Elections BC, Electoral Commission New Zealand, US House Clerk/National Archives, ECI India, Japan House of Representatives, TSE Brazil, South African Parliament and other cited official authorities** — extracted public institutional, party and election facts. Each record links to its source; this project does not assert ownership of those facts or grant broader rights to the original publications.

The combined politics file conservatively retains the IPU-derived dataset's CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms. Separately sourced public facts and geometry retain the terms above. A source citation is not an endorsement. All game-generated polls, events, approval indicators and results are fictional and should not be presented as official data.
